Advanced

Devices added with a link

Each one has its own key. Remove a phone you've lost or given away, and it stops syncing.

    Quick add from Shortcuts

    An iPhone, iPad or Mac Shortcut can put a todo straight into Now without opening the app, from the share sheet, Siri, the Action button or Back Tap. It needs these two things. How to set it up.

    Invite people

    An invite link gives someone their own synced lists on this sync service. It works once and expires after 14 days. The note is only for you, to remember who it's for.

      Delete your board

      This permanently deletes your board, its nightly copies and its access token from the sync service. Your other devices stop syncing. It can't be undone.

      On this device

      Save a copy of all your lists, including Done, to this device. Keep the backup file to restore it later. The CSV file opens in Numbers or Excel.

      Nightly copies

      The sync service saves a copy of your board each night it changes, and at least once a week, and keeps it for 30 days.

      Now or Later

      A notebook for your todos.

      NowCall the dentistPick up milk
      TomorrowReturn library books
      LaterPlan a gardenLearn to knit

      Write down what's on your plate, then sort it. Now is for today, Tomorrow is for next, and Later is for someday.

      How a day works

      • Check it off. Finished todos stay in their list for the rest of the day, then move to Done the next morning.
      • Tomorrow turns into Now. Each morning, anything still in Tomorrow moves into Now. Nothing goes overdue.
      • Make it yours. Double-tap a todo to change its words, or use its ••• menu. Drag todos between lists by their handle. Tap a list's name to rename it, or its colored dot to recolor it. You can have up to 10 lists.

      You can change how mornings work in Settings.

      Your lists, your devices

      • No account needed. Your lists are saved on this device and work offline.
      • Got an invite or device link? Open it on this device and sync sets itself up, so your lists show up everywhere you use Now or Later.
      • Keep it handy. Add it to your Home Screen from Safari's Share button.
